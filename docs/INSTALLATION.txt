JMU COURSE PLANNER — INSTALLATION GUIDE
Start here. No coding or Terminal commands needed.

BEFORE YOU START

You need a Mac running macOS Sonoma 14 or newer. This download works on
Apple silicon and Intel Macs. It does not run on Windows, iPhone, or iPad.

Download only from this project's release page:
https://github.com/kyol-dony/JMU-Course-Selector/releases

This is an unofficial student planning tool, not a JMU registration system.
Confirm your course requirements with your advisor before registering.

WHY YOUR MAC MAY SHOW A WARNING

This app is not notarized by Apple and does not have an Apple Developer ID
signature. Apple has not checked this release for malicious software.
Your Mac may say the developer cannot be verified or Apple cannot check
the app for malicious software. This does not establish that the app is
safe or unsafe. Open it only if you trust this project and its download.

1. DOWNLOAD AND INSTALL

1) On the release page, find Assets. Click the app download whose name
   begins with JMU Course Planner (spaces may appear as dots) and ends
   in .zip. Do not choose "Source code."
2) In Finder, open Downloads. Double-click the .zip if it has not already
   opened. Open the folder it creates.
3) Drag JMU Course Planner.app into Applications in the Finder sidebar.
   Finder may hide the .app ending; that is normal.
4) Open Applications and double-click JMU Course Planner.

2. IF YOUR MAC BLOCKS THE FIRST OPEN

Use these steps only for a developer-verification or Apple-cannot-check
warning, and only for the copy you downloaded from the project above.

1) Close the warning using Done, Cancel, or OK, whichever appears.
   Do not choose Move to Trash if you want to continue installing.
2) Click the Apple menu at the top-left of your screen, then System Settings.
3) Select Privacy & Security in the sidebar. Scroll down to Security.
4) Find the message about JMU Course Planner being blocked.
   Click Open Anyway. On some macOS versions, click Open first to see it.
5) If macOS asks, use Touch ID or enter your Mac login password to confirm.
   You do not need an Apple Account password or a developer account.
6) Click Open if another confirmation appears.

This approves this app specifically. Later, open it normally from
Applications. A new version may need approval again.

Do not disable your Mac's security protections or grant Full Disk Access
to install this app. No security-disabling commands are needed.

3. GET STARTED

Keep an internet connection available while the app loads JMU course data.
Follow setup to choose your major, any additional programs, and prior
credit. Generated schedules are drafts for discussion with your advisor.

If you import a transcript or save an export, select the file or location
you want to use. Do not share transcripts or student IDs in public support
requests. Installation itself needs no camera or microphone access.

IF SOMETHING DOES NOT WORK

No Open Anyway button?
Try opening the app from Applications again, close the warning, then
return to Privacy & Security. Apple makes this option available for about
an hour after an opening attempt. Close and reopen System Settings if needed.

School- or work-managed Mac?
Your organization may block installation. Ask its IT team for help rather
than changing organization security settings.

"Will damage your computer," malware, or "app is damaged" warning?
Stop. These are different from an unidentified-developer warning. Do not
follow the override steps above. Delete that copy and contact the project
maintainer. A fresh download from the official release page may help with
a damaged download; do not force it open if the warning remains.

Need help?
https://github.com/kyol-dony/JMU-Course-Selector/issues
Include your macOS version, app version, and exact warning text.
Leave out your password, transcript, student ID, and other private details.

INSTALLING AN UPDATE

Save your plan in the app, then quit it. Download and unzip the new release.
Drag the new app into Applications and choose Replace if Finder asks.
This replaces the app, not its separately saved plans. Updating does not
require deleting saved plans or course data. Follow first-open approval
again if prompted.
